From one structural scan to a full 4D view of brain function.

CONNECT-4 synthesizes subject-specific resting-state fMRI from T1-weighted structural MRI through connectivity-guided hyperedge fusion.

Explore the method

Salma Hassan · Mostafa Salem · Ayman Elsayed · Olivier Oullier · Mohammad Yaqub

Architecture

Anatomy becomes dynamics.

A unified hypergraph fuses image patches, anatomical regions, connectivity priors and normative context before a diffusion transformer and temporal UNet generate 128-frame rs-fMRI.

Method highlights

Built around biological structure.

CONNECT-4 treats fMRI synthesis as a systems-level prediction problem—not a contrast translation task.

  1. 01

    4D synthesis

    Generate a complete spatiotemporal BOLD sequence from a single T1-weighted scan.

  2. 02

    Hyperedge fusion

    Unify anatomy, ROI masks, connectivity priors and subject-specific normative context.

  3. 03

    Biological objective

    Align voxel signals, regional distributions and functional connectivity across scales.
